# Visualizing the Advantage: Presentation Charts Guide

This document provides a quick overview of the custom visualizations generated by `generate_comparative_charts.py`, what they measure, and the narrative they help you tell to both technical and clinical stakeholders.

## 1. Trade-off Radar Chart (`chart_6_tradeoff_radar.png`)
* **What it shows:** A multi-dimensional comparison of the Baseline (Fuzzy), Gemini Cloud LLM, and our Optimized Local ML pipeline across 5 critical axes: **F1-Score, AUC-ROC, Speed, Privacy, and Cost-Efficiency**.
* **Why it's useful:** It visually demonstrates the "Pareto Frontier" of clinical AI. While the Cloud LLM matches the Custom ML on F1-Score and AUC-ROC, its area on the radar completely collapses on Speed, Privacy, and Cost. It proves that to get a *deployable* system, you needed a custom local architecture.
* **The Narrative:** "We matched the clinical accuracy of a trillion-parameter cloud LLM using a local ensemble, while keeping inference free, lightning-fast, and 100% offline for HIPAA compliance."

## 2. Clinical Impact: Alert Fatigue vs. Missed Threats (`chart_7_alert_fatigue.png`)
* **What it shows:** A translation of the standard ROC curve into real-world hospital metrics: **Nuisance Alerts (False Positives)** vs. **Missed Severe Threats (False Negatives)** per 100 prescriptions.
* **Why it's useful:** Doctors ignore standard ROC curves, but they acutely feel "Alert Fatigue" (too many pop-ups) and the danger of missed fatal interactions. This chart maps the cloud LLM as a single fixed point, while showing that our Local ML ensemble has a *tunable curve*.
* **The Narrative:** "Cloud APIs force you to accept their fixed risk threshold. Because our local ensemble outputs calibrated probabilities, we can dynamically slide our threshold to the exact 'sweet spot' that minimizes alert fatigue without letting a fatal interaction slip through."

## 3. Reliability Calibration Diagram (`chart_8_calibration.png`)
* **What it shows:** A comparison of the models' "Predicted Probability" against the actual "Fraction of True Severe Interactions." A perfectly calibrated model hugs the 45-degree diagonal.
* **Status note:** temperature scalars sit at 1.5 evidence-backed (refit on held-out val NLL moves nothing — see UPDATE_3.md), and the stacker trains on simulated out-of-fold features, so treat this chart as aspirational until stacker-level calibration lands. Day-to-day reliability currently comes from the codebook decision policy, and the chart regenerates with `generate_comparative_charts.py`.

## 4. End-to-End ROC & Pipeline Metrics (`chart_2`, `chart_4`, `chart_5`)
* **What they show:** The standard academic benchmarking metrics (Accuracy, F1, Sensitivity, Specificity, AUC-ROC) comparing the Base Models vs Ensembles, and the Keyword vs Ensemble end-to-end pipelines.
* **Why it's useful:** These are the receipts. They prove the ML rigor behind the project, confirming that techniques like Class Weighting (Focal Loss) and Stacking Generalization genuinely improved minority class detection over naive approaches.
